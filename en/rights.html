About our content

  • The badges, characters, scenes and interface illustrations in this app were made for this app. These illustrations were generated with AI image tools, then selected, edited and arranged by us.
  • Some badges depict real buildings, railway stations, artifacts, musical instruments or natural features so that children can learn about them. To learn their general appearance we read written public information and then composed our own pictures; we did not trace any photo and did not use images whose license is unclear or that do not allow commercial use. A few illustrations used images we checked as usable for reference, namely research figures, museum images and photos that are openly licensed (CC BY, Open Government Data License) or in the public domain. Those that require credit are listed under “Badges based on openly licensed images” below and credited on each badge's page; the rights in those original images remain with their owners.
  • Badge descriptions are written by us from public sources, which are listed under "Badge info".
  • Our claim of rights in this app’s content does not cover the third-party, openly licensed or public-domain materials mentioned above; those materials are used under their own license terms or the law.

Not affiliated

Names of places, buildings, stations, museums, religious sites, companies, brands and instruments are used only to describe and teach. Their mention does not mean that this app is affiliated with, sponsored, licensed or endorsed by them. All names, trademarks and works belong to their respective owners.

Naming the buildings we depict

Some badges depict the exterior of modern buildings that are still in copyright. Under Article 58 of Taiwan's Copyright Act, buildings on long-term display in outdoor places open to the public may be drawn; Article 64 requires the source to be named in a reasonable way.

Every such badge names the building, the year it was completed and the architect, where recorded, on that badge's own info page. The Traditional Chinese version of this page also carries the full list. Where no architect is on record, the owner or managing body is named instead. Buildings whose copyright has expired are not listed.

If anything there is wrong, or you are the architect or another rights holder, please contact us below and we will correct it.

Contact for rights holders

If you own a copyright, trademark or other right and believe something in this app may infringe it, please email:

vamrebirth@gmail.com

Please include, where possible:

  1. Your name or organization, and how to reach you.
  2. The right you are asserting, such as the title of the work or the trademark registration number.
  3. The content concerned: the world and badge name, ideally with a screenshot.
  4. What you would like us to do, such as modify it, remove it or add an attribution.

What we will do:

  • Acknowledge your email within 7 business days.
  • While we look into it, temporarily replace or remove the content in our next update.
  • After review, modify, replace or add attribution as appropriate, and let you know the outcome.

We prefer to resolve any concern through conversation, and we thank you for helping us improve.
