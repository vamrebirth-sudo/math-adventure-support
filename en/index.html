算算探險隊: an arithmetic adventure for kids

算算探險隊 is an iOS math game for children to practise addition, subtraction, multiplication and division. It is free to download on the Taiwan App Store. The first 3 chapters of every topic in every world are free; you can try all 30 worlds free for 7 days, or unlock the full version with a one-time in-app purchase. There are no ads or subscriptions, and no sign-up. The actual price is shown on the App Store; purchases and refunds are handled by Apple under applicable law and Apple’s terms.

What children do

  • Explore themed worlds such as space, dinosaurs, the ocean, ancient civilisations and Taiwanese culture, practising addition, subtraction, multiplication and division along the way.
  • Earn badges for correct answers. Each badge comes with a short knowledge card about animals, artefacts, buildings and cultures.
  • Parents choose which operations are open, how many questions each badge takes, and daily and per-session practice limits.

Designed for parents' peace of mind

  • Practice, pictures and music work offline; an internet connection is needed only when a parent opens an outside website. No ads or trackers.
  • Settings, external websites, purchases and the free trial sit behind a parent lock; nothing is charged when the trial ends.
  • The Parent report shows practice time, accuracy and most-missed topics for the last 7 days, compared with the 7 days before when there is enough data; records on the device are kept for up to 28 days.
  • Available in Traditional Chinese and English.

FAQ

Where is my child's progress stored?
On this device. The app has no backend server, so nothing is uploaded and we never receive any data. Deleting the app deletes the data in the app’s folder; the parent lock setting kept in the iPhone Keychain may not be removed by the system.

What about backup files I export?
A backup file is created only when a parent exports one. Copies saved to Google Drive, iCloud or anywhere else, and iCloud device backups, are not inside the app: deleting the app does not delete them, and we cannot delete them for you. Please delete them yourself when you no longer need them.

How does the free trial work? Will I be charged when it ends?
In the app's Settings (parent check required), open “Purchase & Free Trial” and tap “Start 7-Day Free Trial”. The trial is a free in-app item; Apple records when it starts. After 7 days the app goes back to the free chapters, and nothing is charged or renewed. Each Apple Account can use the trial once, and deleting and reinstalling the app does not restart it.

How do I get my purchase back on a new phone or after reinstalling?
Sign in with the Apple Account you used to buy, then tap “Restore Purchases” in “Purchase & Free Trial”. Apple keeps the purchase record; we never receive your payment details. For refunds, contact Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I clear practice records?
In the app's Settings, choose "Clear all practice records" to clear the currently selected child's records. To remove all of a child's data, delete that child in Settings.
