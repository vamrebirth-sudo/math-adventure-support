Privacy Policy

Applies to: Math Adventure (iOS app, “the App”)

Provided by: The Math Adventure Team

Effective date: November 14, 2026

The App is a math adventure game where children practice addition, subtraction, multiplication and division. We know parents care deeply about their children’s privacy, so we decided from the start: the App does not collect any personal information.

1. Summary

  • No sign-up or sign-in. The App never asks for a child’s name, age, birthday or photo.
  • No personal or device information is collected or uploaded.
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • Learning progress, the parent report and settings are stored on this device. The App has no backend server, so we never receive any data.
  • Outside websites open in the device’s browser only after a parent passes the parent check.

2. Data stored on the device

So that children can pick up where they left off, the App stores the following on the device:

  • Learning progress: completed tasks, stars, collected badges, the current world and chapter, and unfinished answers.
  • Settings: app language, hints, animation, music and sound effects, reading font, enabled operations, tasks per badge, and daily and per-session practice limits.
  • Usage time: daily and per-session practice time and the break countdown, used to remind children to take breaks.
  • Parent report records: time spent in the App and on the practice screen each day, and the result of each “Check answer” (time, world, chapter, and whether it was correct). These are used to show practice time, accuracy and most missed topics in Settings › Parent report. Only the last 28 days are kept; older records are deleted automatically.
  • Parent lock: whether it is on and which verification method is used. A parent PIN is stored only as a one-way hash, so neither we nor anyone else can see the original digits.

This data is never sent to us or to any third party. The App has no backend server and no data-collection service of any kind. Deleting the App deletes the data the App stores on this device; however, backup files a parent has exported (see Section 3) and iCloud backups are not inside the App and are not deleted with it.

Parents can also choose “Clear all practice records” in Settings. This permanently deletes the completion records, stars, badges and parent report records for all worlds from this device. Parent settings are not changed.

If iCloud Backup is turned on for your iPhone, the system may include the App’s data in the backup. This is an Apple feature handled under your Apple Account settings and Apple’s privacy policy; we cannot access or delete it. To delete an iCloud backup, manage it yourself in your iPhone’s Settings.

3. Backup files

Parents can “Export backup” in Settings to save learning progress and settings as a file, then “Import” it on another device.

  • A backup file is created only when a parent chooses to, and the parent decides where to save it (for example the Files app, AirDrop or a cloud drive).
  • Backup files do not include the parent PIN, usage time, the break timer or parent report records.
  • The App never uploads backup files automatically.
  • Once a backup file is saved to a cloud drive (for example Google Drive or iCloud Drive), sent to someone else or stored anywhere else, it is no longer inside the App and we cannot delete it. Please delete it yourself when you no longer need it.

4. Network and outside websites

The App’s games, questions, pictures and music are all inside the App. Playing does not need an internet connection, and the App does not send data in the background. The privacy policy, music credits and font licenses in Settings are also built into the App and can be read offline.

The App has no built-in web browser. Outside websites open only in these cases, and always behind the parent check:

  • A badge’s reading websites, such as pages from museums or science organizations: after passing the parent check and choosing a site, a parent taps “Open in browser”.
  • Track source and license links in the music credits in Settings (opening Settings requires the parent check).

These websites open in the device’s browser (such as Safari). Browsing history, cookies and connection information are then handled by the browser and the website under their own policies; please see each website’s privacy policy. The App never sends any of your child’s data to these websites.

Museums, science organizations and other websites cited by the App are reference sources only. They do not partner with or endorse the App.

5. Device permissions

The App does not ask for access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or notifications, and does not ask to track your activity across other apps or websites.

Exporting and importing backups uses the system share sheet and file picker, which appear only when a parent chooses to use them.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is designed for children and handles privacy in line with Taiwan’s Personal Data Protection Act, the Protection of Children and Youths Welfare and Rights Act, and Apple’s App Store rules for apps in the Kids category:

  • No personal information is collected from children.
  • No ads, and no third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • Outside links and Settings are behind the parent check.

If the App ever needs to handle personal information in the future, we will update this policy first, tell parents clearly in the App, and obtain parental consent where the law requires it.

7. Your rights

Because we do not collect any personal information, there is no data for us to look up, correct or delete. To clear your child’s progress and parent report records on the device, choose “Clear all practice records” in Settings. Deleting the App removes all data the App stores on the device. Backup files a parent has exported and iCloud backups are not deleted with it and need to be deleted by the parent.

If you email us, we will use your email address and message only to answer your question, for no other purpose, and will delete them once the matter is resolved.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish it with an App update. Parents will be notified in the App about significant changes.
